Typing War

Type faster and more accurately to damage your opponent.

How to Play Typing War

Typing War is a real-time 1v1 typing battle. Both players receive the same word and race to complete it. Every successful word gives the player an attack against the opponent.

1
Enter your name

Choose a name and start a Quick Match or create a private room.

2
Get ready

After your opponent joins, both players press I'm Ready to begin.

3
Type the shared word

The current word is synchronized for both players. If either player finishes it, the next word changes for both.

4
Build your combo

Type correctly and quickly to keep your combo and deal more damage.

5
Defeat your opponent

Reduce your opponent's HP to 0 before they defeat you. The battle also has a 60-second time limit.

Typing War Tips

  • Accuracy matters — avoid unnecessary mistakes.
  • Stay focused because the shared word can change as soon as your opponent finishes.
  • Keep your combo going to maximize your attacks.
  • Use Quick Match for an automatic opponent or Private Room to play with a friend.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Typing War multiplayer?

Yes. Typing War is designed as a real-time 1v1 multiplayer typing game.

Do both players get the same word?

Yes. The current word is shared between both players. When one player completes it, the next word is synchronized for both players.

Who deals damage?

The player who successfully completes the current word deals the attack damage. The other player's unfinished input is reset to the new shared word.

How do I play with a friend?

Use Create Private Room, share the room code with your friend, and have them use Join Room.

What happens when a player reaches 0 HP?

The player whose HP reaches 0 is defeated and the opponent is declared the winner.

Can I play a rematch?

Yes. After a battle, both players can choose Rematch. Both players must become ready before the next round starts.
