Speed Rush

Unlike Word Blitz, Speed Rush is a multiplayer word competition. Choose 2–10 players, give everyone the one word at a time, and compete for the highest WPM on the live leaderboard.

How Speed Rush multiplayer works

Create a private room for 2–10 players and share its code, or use Quick Match to find a full group. Everyone receives the same word sequence and starts together. Type one word correctly, then the next word appears immediately. Keep typing until the clock reaches zero. Players are ranked by score, with accuracy and words used as tie-breakers.
