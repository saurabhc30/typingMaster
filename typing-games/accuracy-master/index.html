Accuracy Master

The typing battle where precision matters more than raw speed.

How to Play

1. Enter your name and choose Quick Match or a Private Room.
2. When your opponent connects, both players press I'm Ready.
3. Both players receive the exact same paragraph and type directly in the passage.
4. Every wrong character costs 5 HP; correct characters turn green and the expected character for a mistake turns red.
5. If you stop typing for 5 seconds, an inactivity penalty starts. After that, every 6 seconds without typing costs 5 HP, then 10 HP, then 15 HP, increasing by 5 HP each cycle. Typing any character resets this penalty cycle.
6. Completing the passage does not cause an instant win. The first finisher starts a 5-second finish window. The final performance score decides the winner.

Scoring

Final score = Accuracy 45% + Progress 30% + WPM 15% + HP 10%. WPM is capped at 120 for scoring. Finishing first only starts the 5-second finish window; it does not automatically win the round. If both players have the same score, the result is a draw.

Important Rules

Type directly in the passage. Backspace and Delete do nothing and never cause HP damage. Paste, drag/drop, and text corrections are disabled. Every newly typed wrong character costs 5 HP, and lost HP never comes back. Inactivity rule: after 5 seconds without typing, HP is reduced every 6 seconds by 5 HP, then 10 HP, then 15 HP, increasing by 5 HP each cycle. Typing any character immediately resets the inactivity cycle. Finishing first starts a 5-second finish window; final score decides the winner.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the winner decided?

Completing the passage first starts a 5-second finish window; it does not automatically decide the match. After the window, or when both players finish, the final score is compared: 45% accuracy, 30% progress, 15% WPM, and 10% remaining HP. If one player reaches 0 HP, that player loses immediately. If an opponent disconnects or leaves, the remaining player wins.

Do both players get the same text?

Yes. The host selects one paragraph and sends that exact paragraph to both players. Both players type against identical text.

How long is each round?

Each round gets a time limit based on the length of the selected passage. Finishing the passage early starts a 5-second finish window so the opponent can finish too.

What happens when I make a mistake?

The expected character in the passage turns red, the error count increases, and 5 HP is removed. The wrong character itself is never inserted into the passage.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. Create a Private Room and share the room code with your friend.

Can I rematch?

Yes. After the result screen, both players can choose Rematch and confirm readiness for another round.

What happens if my opponent leaves?

If your opponent disconnects or leaves during the match, you are awarded the victory.

Can I use Backspace?

No. Backspace and Delete are completely disabled. Clicking them does not remove a character and does not reduce HP. You must continue from the current typing position.

Is WPM more important than accuracy?

No. Accuracy is 45% of the final score. Progress is 30%, WPM is 15%, and remaining HP is 10%.
