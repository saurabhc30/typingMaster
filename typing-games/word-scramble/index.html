How to play Word Scramble

Choose a difficulty and timer, then rearrange the scrambled letters in your head and type the correct word. Correct answers increase your score and combo. Keep typing accurately to improve your WPM and beat your personal best.

Word Scramble FAQ

Is Word Scramble free?

Yes. TypingMeter Word Scramble is free to play in a web browser.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The game is designed to fit small mobile screens without horizontal scrolling.
