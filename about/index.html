About TypingMeter

A minimalist, fast, and feature-rich online platform designed to measure, analyze, and boost your typing speed and precision.

🎨 Custom Experience

How WPM is Calculated

Standard typing speed measurements count 5 characters (including spaces and punctuation) as 1 standard word. TypingMeter calculates speed using the formula:

WPM = ( ( Correct Characters / 5 ) / Time in Minutes )

Raw speed includes all keystrokes typed (both correct and incorrect), while Accuracy reflects the percentage of clean keystrokes relative to total input.
