How to Play Boss Mode

  1. Choose a difficulty and battle length, then start the fight.
  2. Type the attack word exactly and press Enter to hit the monster.
  3. Longer and more complex words give you a damage bonus. Nightmare mode has a tougher boss and stronger attacks.
  4. When the monster launches an attack, a red dodge prompt appears. Type that word before its timer runs out.
  5. If you miss the dodge, your health drops. Defeat the boss before your health or the timer reaches zero.

Tips for winning

  • Prioritize accuracy—one clean, difficult word can be worth more than several easy words.
  • When a dodge prompt appears, switch to it immediately; the attack word can wait.
  • Use Easy to learn the rhythm, then try Hard or Nightmare for a faster, more demanding battle.

Boss Mode FAQ

How do difficult words deal more damage?

Each successful attack has base damage, plus a bonus for word length and difficulty. Harder words are worth more, but they may take longer to type.

What happens if I fail to dodge?

The monster hits you and removes health. If your health reaches zero, the battle ends.

Does Boss Mode save my score?

Your best damage score is saved in this browser on your device. No account is required.

Is Boss Mode free?

Yes. Boss Mode is free to play on TypingWebsite in a modern browser.

How to play

  1. Start the game and type the letters in a monster's word.
  2. Your current word is highlighted in green as you type.
  3. Finish a word to fire at that monster. Bosses need multiple hits.
  4. Stop monsters reaching the bottom. Each one that escapes costs a life.

Improve typing speed with Boss Mode

Boss Mode turns typing practice into an arcade challenge. Read the words, type accurately, and switch targets as monsters descend. The game rewards quick recognition as well as accurate typing.
